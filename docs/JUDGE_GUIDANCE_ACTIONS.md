## Product story and audience

**Demonstration:** change a space-system design, identify which evidence needs rechecking, rerun affected calculations, inspect changed findings and retain unchanged results with provenance. Nemotron investigates the connected evidence and explains concrete next checks; the maintained engineering kernel owns numerical findings.

Student rocketry/CubeSat teams and small space-engineering teams are candidate first audiences. Their needs and willingness to use 6D remain hypotheses. Record actual conversations, observed task difficulties and repeat-use interest before claiming validation. Do not fabricate quotes, users, customer projects or human engineering review.

## Build D acceptance focus

Within the already agreed project-wide investigation scope:

- Answer a representative whole-project question using the exact saved revision, requirement findings, related models, changes and evidence gaps.
- Explain why a finding changed, identify affected versus reused evidence, prioritize unresolved issues and propose specific engineering next checks with traceable citations.
- Preserve deterministic numerical verdicts, explicit exclusions and insufficient-evidence states. An AI summary cannot turn incomplete engineering coverage into mission approval.
- Show the actual provider/model and live/recorded status. Record source context, latency and measured/reserved cost distinctly. Enable a stronger model only after current task evidence justifies it.
- Evaluate supported cases, unsupported requests, contradictory or missing evidence and explanation errors. Preserve failed runs and label offline checks separately from live qualification and human review.

These are planned acceptance outcomes for D, not a claim that D has shipped. See [HACKATHON_BUILD_SCOPE.md](HACKATHON_BUILD_SCOPE.md).

## Submission experience to verify

1. A judge can reach a populated, explicitly constructed example through the protected hosted entry planned in F.
2. The entrant can explain the original finding, make a change, preview dependencies, save/rerun and identify the changed criterion and reused evidence.
3. A project-wide Nemotron investigation adds a concrete, cited next check. When a reference is missing, the Tavily search/review/save path is visible and honestly labelled.
4. A report preserves exact configuration, findings, numerical support, origins and unresolved scope. No readiness or launch-success guarantee is implied.
5. A person outside the project can complete the selected task with minimal assistance. Record observed time and confusion instead of assuming usability from screenshots.
6. The public README, demo/video and feedback describe what actually works and identify constructed data, model limits and incomplete validation.

No completion percentage or 10/10 promise substitutes for these observed outcomes.
