## Behavior

New Assistant investigations carry an explicit `application_changes_v1` policy.
6D derives before/after scalar directions from captured revision evidence, using
finite values with identical units and matching quantity identities. Declared
limits, calculated metrics and named inputs are separate. Missing values,
incompatible units and changed metric identities remain not comparable. A changed
criterion operator also prevents a comparable declared-limit direction. There
is no implicit unit conversion or inference of physical cause/model equivalence.

Exact before/after values, source pointers, source digest and scope stay in the
saved local investigation. The AI receives qualitative directions before writing
commentary; numerical values remain withheld from that commentary context. The
application rederives context at acceptance and blocks inconsistent scope,
tampered context and supported forms of contradictory directional claims. New
saved investigations display the source-bound change table before AI prose.

The guard checks bounded English assertions such as an increased input, a decreased
limit or an unchanged metric. Questions, conditional review guidance and proposed
actions are distinguished from factual recorded changes. Static comparisons
between different quantities are not treated as revision directions. This is not
a general semantic parser and does not establish that unflagged commentary is
correct. Quotation marks alone do not exempt an unsupported factual claim.

Historical receipts without the new marker keep their original policy,
explanations and recorded acceptance. No historical completion or score was
silently corrected. The earlier Super error is rejected only in a separate
offline reassessment under the new policy; Ultra's correct explanation passes
that reassessment. No new live-model success is claimed.
