## Talk through the rocket

Click **Ask 6D**, choose **Ask Nemotron**, then choose the whole project or a specific saved check. Your question and the accepted answer appear as a conversation. Exact calculated status/value/limit remain application-owned, and the longer evidence/activity record opens under **Evidence and investigation details**.

Type a question in your own words. **Enter** sends; **Shift + Enter** adds a line. Follow up in the same conversation, or choose **New conversation**. **Saved conversations** reopens recorded turns without sending a request. Old answers and failures keep their original text and evidence.

Follow-ups use up to three prior complete question/answer pairs, at most 6,000 text characters. Earlier turns can remain visible in the loaded conversation while only recent context is sent. The server validates the prior record's integrity and exact project, saved revision, calculation, scope, selected test row and reference snapshot. A changed evidence context starts fresh; it does not silently attach another context's answer. Earlier prose is untrusted conversation, never new engineering evidence or permission to change a result.

## Pick the model

The selector remains visible and remembers your choice in this browser:

| Model | Suggested use |
| --- | --- |
| Nano | A quick explanation or one narrow evidence question |
| Super | Ordinary review and follow-up questions |
| Ultra | A deeper question involving related findings or conflicting evidence |

These are task suggestions, not universal quality rankings. Selection sends nothing, never automatically escalates, and does not change the server's Super default, historical model labels or shared accounting.

## Images: local preview and your notes

Choose **Add image** for a still PNG, JPEG or WebP. The preview stays in browser memory. Paste visible text or describe what you want to discuss, then ask your question.

**Nemotron receives your description, not the image.** These exact [Nano](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-30b-a3b), [Super](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b) and [Ultra](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b) models have text input. No vision model or OCR is silently substituted. Image notes are unverified user text; they cannot alter a calculated finding or become numerical evidence.

Limits: one still image per context, 5 MiB, at most 8,192 pixels per edge and 12 megapixels; 1,000 characters of notes and 2,000 characters for the complete submitted question/notes. The cache holds at most four contexts in memory, with no permanent image storage. Closing/reopening preserves a local preview; refresh clears it. Send waits for image checking and requires notes for a present preview. Successful job submission/New conversation clears the local attachment; a failed submission retains it.

## Spelling and corrections

Nemotron questions, image notes and Tavily queries enable native English spellchecking. Right-click an underlined word and choose a correction. 6D does not rewrite text automatically, enable spelling on keys, or change browser settings. Underlines/suggestions depend on your browser and dictionary; enhanced browser checking can use the browser vendor's service.
