## How to read the history

- **Git records** establish what was committed and when. Several milestones were developed locally and committed together later; a commit date is not necessarily a feature's delivery date.
- **Dated milestone documents** record that milestone's reported acceptance, results, build identity and limits. Their older counts, caps, selected models and pending statements are historical, not current settings.
- **Plans and acceptance criteria** express intent. They do not establish implementation, a successful test or customer validation.
- **Private raw records** remain local: project databases, keys, ledgers, provider receipts, detailed logs and personal screenshots. Public summaries identify their scope and, where already documented, receipt IDs. This audit does not expose private records or turn a summary into an independent verification.
- The earliest tracked commit is October 14. Documents reporting work on October 10–13 are preserved, but the Git history does not independently reconstruct a pre-October-14 source timeline. Workspace-folder dates are not proof of implementation dates.

## Completeness and remaining gaps

This audit provides an index for **every published file at the baseline** and a feature/decision chronology covering the preserved earlier work. It does not claim every unsaved conversation, discarded experiment or private artifact has a complete public transcript. Missing evidence is identified rather than reconstructed as fact.

- Pre-October-14 source evolution is not independently established by Git. Preserve the dated first-increment claim and disclose that distinction when describing prior work.
- Raw private test/provider receipts and workspace activity are retained locally; public summaries are not a byte-for-byte publication of those records. Customer/user input requires its own consent and data handling.
- No independent human engineering validation, completed outsider usability sessions, customer commitment or real flight validation is established by this record. [Discovery brief](CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY.md).
- F public deployment and its operational/live checks, broader live/human AI evaluation, final owner-led UI/UX refinement/walkthrough, external evidence and submission materials remain open. Ten bounded models do not complete all future 6D capabilities. [Current scope](HACKATHON_BUILD_SCOPE.md).
- Automatic general CAD/code ingestion, coupled high-fidelity disciplines, full launch-to-landing qualification and a production multi-user platform are not implied by the long-term feature catalogue.

Future completed milestones should update this chronology and the relevant guide, acceptance/result evidence, current scope and validation index, then be privacy-checked and pushed. Preserve older records as dated history instead of silently replacing their outcomes.
