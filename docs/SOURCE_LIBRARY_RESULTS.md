## Limits

References and quotations are user-supplied. URLs are stored and opened only on the user's explicit navigation; they are not fetched, authenticated or monitored by this feature. **Linked** means tracked context matches, not that the cited material is relevant, accurate or sufficient. Manual confirmation does not change a finding, resolve unsupported coverage, authenticate an approval or authorize a mission.

The library is local and project-scoped, with at most 100 sources, 300 links and 20 versions per source/link. It supports adding/editing references and confirming context reviews; link retirement/deletion and arbitrary document ingestion are not implemented. It does not inject references into an AI prompt. Tavily retrieval, LangSmith tracing and source-grounded AI interpretation remain separate work. Existing provenance labels and historical engineering receipts retain their original meaning.
